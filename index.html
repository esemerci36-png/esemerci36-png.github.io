Forgotten Science

Scientists who were right too early.

Forgotten Science is a YouTube Shorts channel about the history of science: the people whose discoveries were ignored, mocked or rejected before the world caught up. One short, sourced story a day.

Episodes are researched and written by the channel owner. Narration, animation and captions are produced with our own software, which uploads each finished video to our own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.
